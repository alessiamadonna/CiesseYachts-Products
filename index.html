Deck fittings

Parts we designed ourselves,
produced in Viareggio

CIESSE Yachts is an Italian company based in Viareggio. We draw our own range of marine fittings, register the designs and produce them here: printing, engraving and finishing all happen in the same workshop.

Our own patents

The cabin and deck ranges come from our own registered designs and are produced nowhere else.

Catalogue, not bespoke

Made in Italy

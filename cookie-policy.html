2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in the browser of the user. Similar techniques, such as local storage, allow information to be saved in the same way. This policy uses the word cookie for both.

3. Technical cookies (no consent required)

This site uses only the following technical entries, saved in the local storage of the browser and never sent to third parties:

Name Purpose Duration
ciesse-consent Stores the choice made in the cookie banner and the date of the choice 6 months

These entries are strictly necessary to provide the service requested by the user and, under article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code, do not require consent.

4. Third party cookies (consent required)

Service Purpose Provider and policy
Google Maps Shows the interactive map on the contact page. Google receives the IP address of the user and may set its own cookies. Google Ireland Ltd – policies.google.com/privacy

The map is not loaded until the user gives consent. Until then, the page shows a static notice in its place. No analytics, profiling or advertising cookies are used on this site.

5. Giving, refusing and withdrawing consent

On the first visit a banner allows the user to accept, refuse or select single categories. Closing the banner with the X does not amount to consent and no third party service is loaded. The choice is stored for six months, after which the banner is shown again; it is also shown again if the services in use change. Consent can be changed at any time through the link Cookie preferences in the footer of every page.

6. Browser settings

Cookies already stored can also be deleted from the settings of the browser in use (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Disabling technical cookies may affect the correct display of the pages.

7. Contact

For any request concerning this policy write to info@ciesseyachts.it. Further information on the processing of personal data is available in the privacy policy.
